# AI Policy

UQ Computing Society bans the use of generative AI for any purpose in all our repositories.
On top of the plethora of ethical problems associated with generative AI and its usage,
these tools pose a serious risk to the stability of our digital infrastructure
and security of the personal data of society members.
Leveraging generative AI would also severely harm the trust our members hold in the committee and our services.

To outline more explicitly (but not exhaustively), generative AI must not be used to:

- Produce repository artifacts (code, documentation, prose, images, videos, audio, etc.)
- Review pull requests
- File or comment on issues or pull requests
- Ingest the repository or artifacts derived from the repository (e.g. summarisation, agentic tools)
- Create or interact with deployments

These rules apply to all contributors regardless of whether they are in committee or a member of the society.

Maintainers are not obligated to review, merge or interact with issues or pull requests they suspect to be created using generative AI.
Maintainers may ask you additional questions about the nature and content of your submission to determine if you are in fact the author.
Repeated or particularly agregious use of generative AI may result in being banned from contributing to
that particular repository or all UQCS repositories.

Please note that if you harrass maintainers for exercising their judgement,
your behaviour will dealt with in accordance with the [UQCS Code of Conduct](https://github.com/UQComputingSociety/code-of-conduct/blob/main/README.md).
